A05 - Asyncio Execution and Bounded Concurrency

PROGRAM::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
We're going to keep the domain continuity from A02–A04 and build a:

Concurrent Market Fixture Ingestion Pipeline

No network. No live API. No Trader integration.

You'll have several controlled market-data fixtures representing independent incoming observations:

AAPL fixture ─┐
MSFT fixture ─┼──► concurrent ingestion
NVDA fixture ─┤
AMD fixture  ─┤
GOOG fixture ─┘

Each fixture will simulate an I/O-bound operation with different delays.

Conceptually:

Producer(s)
    │
    ▼
┌──────────────┐
│ BOUNDED QUEUE│
└──────┬───────┘
       │
       ├────► Worker 1
       ├────► Worker 2
       └────► Worker 3

The important word is bounded.

If producers can create work faster than consumers can process it, we're not going to allow:

Queue size:

10
100
1,000
100,000
1,000,000
...

to grow forever.

That gives us our first practical exposure to backpressure.
